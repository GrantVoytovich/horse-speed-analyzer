Horse Motion Analyzer

Computer-vision gait & speed analysis

Upload a video of a horse and the app finds and tracks it frame by frame, then measures how it's moving — relative speed, gait, and stride cadence. It works straight from your phone with no calibration or markers: speed is read in the horse's own body lengths, not miles per hour.

    Why BL/s
    Without the camera distance, true mph can't be recovered from pixels alone. Measuring movement in the horse's own body lengths keeps the speed read consistent whether it's filmed up close or far away — ideal for comparing clips and spotting changes in a horse's motion.
